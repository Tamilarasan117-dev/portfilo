# Premium UI Design Skill

## Overview
This skill defines the visual language, typography, HUD telemetry, and interaction design for the TAMILARASAN portfolio.

## Core Responsibilities
- **Cyberpunk / Sci-Fi Aesthetic**:
  - Color palette: Deep obsidian backgrounds (`#07080d`, `#0b0f19`), Cyan neon (`#00f0ff`), Electric Purple (`#8a2be2`), Hot Pink (`#ff0055`), Amber warning (`#ffaa00`), and Pure White (`#ffffff`).
  - Typography: `Orbitron` for display titles and badges, `Rajdhani` for HUD data and subtitles, and `Inter` for long-form reading content.
- **HUD & Telemetry**:
  - Live digital speedometer (km/h) and tachometer arcs.
  - Interactive mini-map showing player car position and 7 district landmark beacons.
  - Keyboard/touch control guide pills (W/A/S/D or Arrow keys, ESC, Camera modes).
- **Portfolio District Panels**:
  - Glassmorphic modal cards (`backdrop-blur-md`, subtle borders `rgba(255,255,255,0.1)`, dark transluscent surface).
  - Clean categorization for About, Education, Skills, Projects, Experience, Achievements, and Contact Garage.
  - Responsive layout ensuring readability on mobile, tablet, and ultra-wide displays.
